## Why execution was withheld

The canonical [integrity playbook, section 7A](../../../agent-skill-documents/EXPERIMENT_INTEGRITY_SKILLS.md)
states: **“If a strict enforceable dollar maximum is required, do not send the
Pro request.”** Its documented historical Pro receipts exceeded per-request
token counts, and it explicitly distinguishes a reserve estimate from a
provider billing cap. The canonical helper's client guard is therefore
insufficient to guarantee the user's exact ceiling.

Current official sources were opened and checked on 17 September:

- The [Astra model page](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-astra)
  and [latest-model guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/latest-model)
  identify `gpt-6-astra` and support Pro through the Responses API.
- The [reasoning guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/reasoning)
  specifies `reasoning.mode="pro"`, with effort chosen separately. Pro bills
  aggregate model work at standard token rates.
- [Pricing](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing) lists Astra standard
  short-context input/cache-write/output at [amount redacted]/12.50/50 per million tokens;
  long-context rates are [amount redacted]/25/75. These rates support an estimate, not
  a guaranteed maximum bill.
- [Spend limits](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/spend-limits)
  explicitly warns that enforcement is not instantaneous and recorded spending
  can exceed a configured hard limit. No documented per-request dollar cap
  establishing a strict [amount redacted] guarantee was found.

We also checked for an existing signed-in ChatGPT subscription path that
would allow the exact requested model and Pro mode at zero incremental cost.
The available browser inventory returned `apps: []` and `browsers: []`.
No authenticated UI or included model/mode could be established. Nothing
was submitted through a browser and no account setting was changed.

No API key was loaded: the unresolved spending boundary precedes credential
access. API account/model entitlement was consequently not tested. A local
credential-free dry run can preserve the exact request and estimate, but is
not a consultation and does not authorize replacing the hard maximum with
an expected cost. The research continues locally without waiting for Pro.
