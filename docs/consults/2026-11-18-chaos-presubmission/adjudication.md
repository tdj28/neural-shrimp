# Final scientific review of the fold and escape claims

On 18 November 2026 the author approved one GPT-6.2 Astra Pro consultation on selected excerpts from the *Chaos* manuscript at journal commit `628890cb2d4815775c2d641c6f916c750ccb9035`. It covered the abstract, the two prospective fold tests, the return-and-capture studies, the periodic-orbit certificates and the conclusion. The reviewer recommended revision before submission. The judgments below come from checking its objections against the recorded measurements.

| Finding | Evidence and disposition |
| --- | --- |
| B01. Fold forecasts in the abstract and conclusion | Accepted. Recounting all 48 measurements gives 23/24 within one return-map iterate for the first primary forecast (the discrete map) and 18/24 for the second (the integral). Each test required all 24. The integral gave 24/24 as a secondary forecast in the first stage. Its pooled 42/48 is retained in the results with that qualification. All six second-stage misses were at the period-6 fold, where measured passages were shorter than forecast. |
| B02. Interpreting the expansion | Accepted. All 26 complete tangent windows showed positive growth. They came from 15 trajectories, 13 subsequently matched to period three. The revised escape section describes expansion before capture as consistent with transient chaos. The evidence allows a long expanding transient followed by periodic capture. |
| B03. Interpreting the escape rate | Accepted. The matrix predicts a long-run escape probability of 0.0130 per departure for its quasi-stationary distribution. Recounting the 48 fresh histories gives 42 endings in 2417 departures with known outcomes (0.0174). The pooled rate includes early transients and gives greater weight to longer histories. Six trajectories were censored. The original scores were 40 period-three approaches and two other candidates. A later check placed all 42 final states near the same orbit. The original scoring labels and freeze chronology are retained. |
| S01. What closely spaced exits predict | Accepted. Each displaced exit was followed for one cycle. A difference in next event or return time is the paired outcome. Variation in the number of returns before capture was observed separately in the natural trajectory ensemble. |
| S02. Auxiliary forecasts | Accepted. The 35.6 expected endings use the departure bins actually visited. The 1071/1074 timing score covers returns on the two previously identified smooth branches and starts each forecast at the observed departure. Individual-capture misses, false alarms and the always-return baseline remain explicit. |
| S03. Scope of the proofs | Accepted. The abstract and cover letter restrict local attraction to the fixed-prefix 0.5B map at the certified parameter settings. The proof section retains its limits on local uniqueness and independent enclosure checks. |
| R01. References | Checked the ten cited entries named in the review against publisher-deposited DOI records. Kim's preprint reports the two amplitude predictions from local derivatives. The historical interval citations now distinguish verified periodic orbits from computer-assisted proofs of chaos. The paper gives a short derivation of the discrete passage-count correction. |
| F01. Figures | Checked Figs. 18 and 19 in the rendered article. Fig. 18 distinguishes complete and truncated growth windows. In Fig. 19b the axis now includes changes in time as well as outcome. Fig. 19c compares survival curves. The blue band comes from the matrix training data. The prespecified comparison to the pooled ending fraction is reported in the text. |
