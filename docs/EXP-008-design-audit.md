# EXP-008 prospective spatial-design and feature audit

Date: 2026-09-13. Scope: `hopfield_information_design.py` and its synthetic
tests, before new trajectory execution. This audit applies the repository's
research policy and canonical integrity sections 4 and 4A. No new neural
trajectory, certificate, preview or waveform outcome was generated here.

## Question and matched comparison

The intended contrast is the usable contribution of old-atlas parameter
geometry to a frozen learner that already receives local diagnostics and
short forecasts. All predictor arms must share starting states, action sets,
native weight doses, previews, scored windows and development information.
Local period and transverse Floquet stability concern the orbit at fixed
weights; they must not be exclusive features of the geometry arm. The root
protocol also supplies crop inclusion and signed rectangle clearance to the
baseline, preventing geographic coverage flags from becoming an information
advantage available only to the atlas representation.

The six added features are deterministic functions of the old atlas, known
coordinates and the candidate action. A positive result can demonstrate useful
representation for this learner and task. It cannot establish theoretically
new information beyond those coordinates, shrimp-specific topology, or a
universal superiority of geometric control. A null or higher regret remains
a valid outcome. Full candidate outcomes must be retained to define regret;
no candidate, anchor or failure may be replaced after new outcomes.

## Selection that can be inspected without new outcomes

The atlas is the same immutable EXP-004 focus map. The selector verifies its
old receipt and consumed files, binds both complete EXP-006 and EXP-007
anchor manifests, and consumes no prior waveform scores. Development uses
all twelve EXP-007 origins, preserving their identities. New test identities
`information-00` through `information-11` are disjoint from them.

Eligibility requires membership in the core's eight-connected nine-peak
component, a nearest observed opposite-component pixel strictly closer than
the nearest crop edge, and normalized Euclidean distance strictly greater
than 0.04 from each of the 24 previous starting origins. The closed halo is
excluded. Normalization uses the complete observed focus widths; all geometry
feature distances and action radii remain in native connection-weight units.
The atlas is never relabeled, and no padding outside its crop is invented.

There are 4,824 core-component cells and 4,714 crop-eligible cells. The old-origin
halos exclude 1,662 crop-eligible cells, leaving 3,052 eligible test pixels.
The selector recursively splits along the widest normalized coordinate near
a weighted median, keeping equal-coordinate pixels together. The requested
number of descendant leaves determines each split's allocation. Alpha wins
equal-span ties; the smaller cut wins equal allocation errors. Each of twelve
leaves chooses its centroid-nearest pixel, breaking ties by row and column.
If the prescribed number of blocks cannot be constructed, selection fails;
it does not reduce the halo or replace missing blocks.

All block memberships, paths, eleven split thresholds, bounds and centroids
are returned in the source packet. The block sizes are
`241,259,271,247,249,254,251,252,250,259,261,258`. Selected row/column pairs are
`(40,31),(35,43),(52,48),(62,48),(73,48),(93,42),(36,71),(49,73),(75,70),
(55,92),(63,120),(76,154)`. The minimum selected distance to any prior origin
is 0.04044505494044407; the minimum test-anchor pair distance is
0.05555555555555555, both in normalized selection units.

These are partitions of eligible pixels with holes, not entirely untouched
rectangular regions. Halos exclude prior origins, not every previous
intervention destination or trajectory. The atlas itself was already seen.
Compact blocks and their bootstrap remain descriptive units for this
conditional sample; the design does not make them independent populations.

## Six parameter-space features and their limits

The fixed order is `anchor_raw_margin`, `destination_signed_pixel_margin`,
`destination_in_core_component`, `outward_projection`,
`destination_inside_crop`, `destination_margin_crop_censored`.

The signed destination margin uses its nearest observed atlas pixel, with
half-pixel ties at the lower axis index. Magnitude is that pixel's raw Euclidean
distance to the nearest observed pixel of the opposite component class. It is
positive inside the core component and negative outside that component;
another disconnected period-nine component is outside this particular one.
The outward projection is the candidate unit direction dotted with the unit
vector from the anchor to its nearest observed non-core pixel. Row/column order
resolves equal-distance normal ties. This definition has no waveform input.

A destination outside the crop has unavailable membership and margin, retained
as `null` in diagnostics. Their numeric feature entries are zero, accompanied
by inside-crop zero and censoring one. Such entries are not known negative
period labels. Within the crop, a pixel margin reaching the destination's
nearest crop-edge distance is flagged as censored; unobserved geometry could
be nearer. The actual signed crop clearance, nearest pixel, normal and feature
availability are retained. Pixel distances are discretized proxies, not exact
bifurcation boundaries, guaranteed control margins or state-space basin radii.

## Prospective phase correction and checks

The first proposed indices, 675 and 2025 of 2,700 endpoint-excluded samples,
were antipodal. Before any new trajectory, review identified near-duplicate
phases under the odd-ODE symmetry in ten of twelve old development cycles.
The prospective phase rule is therefore **675 and 1350**, separated by one
quarter cycle, at unchanged trial cost. Both development and test use this
same rule. This change is based on old evidence and precedes new outcomes;
it is not an outcome-selected replacement.

Eight synthetic tests pass. They independently check complete disjoint block
membership, centroid representatives, split rules, every prior-origin halo,
the closed-halo boundary, no fallback, outcome independence, immutable old
arrays, native anisotropic distances, deterministic normals, half-pixel ties,
disconnected-component labels, explicit outside-crop missingness, invalid
inputs, and exact source-file hashes. An actual old-data-only packet was
compiled successfully; no new integration was used.

The design helper and feature contract are ready for prospective integration.
This scoped audit does not yet certify the separate model-fitting, executor,
failure-census or end-to-end verification code. Those require their own local
checks before the complete source and plan are frozen and pushed.
