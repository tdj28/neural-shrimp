## Contribution search boundary

The current evidence rejects “first shrimps in neural networks” and “first period doubling during training” as contribution statements. The sharper question is whether verified **parameter-window organization** contributes held-out predictive or control value beyond equally funded scalar, recurrence, local-stability, and ordinary performance-map methods. No absence claim about this narrower contribution is made yet.

Before a publication novelty claim, extend the search to adaptive optimization based on recurrence, parameter-space bifurcation control, robust hyperparameter selection, chaotic optimizer meta-losses, fixed-point solvers with damping and acceleration, and early stopping in recurrent reasoning. Check citation chains and newer versions. The present repository is designed to test the question even if related methods already exist.
