# EXP-022: cycle-reference matching feasibility

21 September 2026. Exploratory reference-only proposal, informed by EXP-019/020/021 and the previously committed [advisory design](next-cycle-reference-design.md). **Closed at historical input preparation:** the fixed selector supplies four of six slots; both core candidate slots have no historical match. No target integration was launched, and the proposed 114-job numerical experiment was not frozen for execution. The numerical design below records the prospective draft and reusable adapter work, not a completed neural experiment. See [results](EXP-022-results.md).

**Human-friendly TLDR:** The near-edge network can preserve an earlier input in a form that a simple readout misses. We now test whether other periodic cycles can be compared at similar contraction and input visibility. Suitable references would let a later experiment ask whether ordinary dynamics explain the memory difference. Missing matches or numerical failures are informative limits, not permission to replace candidates until a favorable comparison appears.

## Question, expectation and claim boundary

The primary question is whether the fixed six-cycle, eight-phase panel supplies two numerically qualified reference pairs inside the declared contraction, period and immediate-detectability calipers. Prior memory results motivate the expectation that ordinary contraction and observable transport can explain much of the behavior. This study evaluates the availability of comparators, not that behavioral prediction itself. Its strongest conclusion is reference-matching feasibility for this finite panel; failure to find common support or qualify a planned reference counts against proceeding with these proposed pairs.

The units are six selected cycle slots on two related gamma paths. Two slots are repeated historical anchors; eight fresh common phase fractions supply new phase measurements, not new independent networks or paths. This is not formal preregistration, independent shrimp geometry verification, a capacity measurement, a trained-network result or a test of a geometry-causal effect. The full A/B Gram geometry and Gaussian noise already determine the reference mixture prediction; an additional geometry effect on that same mathematical quantity is not a meaningful estimand.

## Descriptors, matching and numerical resolution

Save period, both signed/complex transverse multipliers, per-cycle contraction, and physical-model-time log rates only where numerically resolved. Save phase-dependent immediate activation detectability, first/second A/B observable responses and Gram triples at 2T/4T/8T, finite-time state/activation operator gains, and mode/write/read orientation. Epsilon and sigma retain the numerical value 1e-4 in state and activation-observation coordinates respectively. No outcome-dependent amplitude or direction adjustment is allowed.

The planned same-bin pairs are core-rho025 versus failed-near-edge-rho025 and core-rho050 versus failed-near-edge-rho050. Both refined dominant modes must remain resolved and positive; the larger of the two observed method deviations from the target plus the heuristic resolution radius must be at most the exact caliper 1/40. Interpret the saved binary64 values as exact rationals for that containment comparison. Require relative period difference at most 2%, using the larger period as denominator. Require the symmetric difference of eight-phase RMS immediate detectability at most 10%, divided by max(left,right,1e-12). If both detectabilities are below 1e-6, mark insufficient detectability. These fixed engineering calipers define feasibility; they are not useful-effect or significance thresholds.

Compare the two methods' transverse modes by the smaller total two-mode assignment cost, with a lexicographic permutation tie break. Each paired mode's resolution radius is the maximum of its two-method displacement and the two arithmetic floors `64 * eps * max(1, spectral matrix norm) * max(1, eigenvector condition)`. A nonfinite floor makes that mode unresolved. This is a heuristic sensitivity diagnostic, not a rigorous error enclosure or a replacement for stability/trajectory gates. A nonzero mode is resolved only when both method moduli exceed its radius; a positive real mode additionally has exactly zero imaginary part and real part greater than its radius in both methods. The dominant mode must have its smaller method modulus minus its radius strictly above the other mode's larger modulus plus its radius. An unresolved small second multiplier is retained without an invented logarithm. The matching gate concerns the dominant mode only; secondary sign, magnitude and orientation differences remain reported descriptors, without an undeclared secondary-match caliper.

Check all fifteen cycle pairs for numerical loop similarity. Use the 8192-node normalized full-state loop, FFT cyclic alignment followed by 64 fixed golden-section steps within one grid interval using periodic cubic-Hermite state/flow interpolation; retain a 4096/8192 interpolation diagnostic. No time reversal or warp is allowed. Per-coordinate scale is max(1,maxabs of both loops). Period-relative difference <=1e-8 and aligned scaled-state RMS <=1e-8 define numerical near-identity. Same exact alpha/gamma plus near-identity is a duplicate that fails distinctness. Cross-parameter near-identity is reported without claiming proven orbit identity or rejecting the pair solely for similarity. Retain unresolved comparisons; an unresolved same-parameter identity cannot certify distinctness.

Full matching feasibility requires all six references, all 48 two-method phase contexts, both planned pairs and required distinctness checks to pass. The report distinguishes complete ledger execution, numerical qualification and this feasibility decision. No favorable subset supplies the full-panel verdict or a new memory mean. Missing/blocked rows remain in all denominators.
