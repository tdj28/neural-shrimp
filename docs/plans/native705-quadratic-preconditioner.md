Prepared 11 October 2026, after measured maintained continuation. The affine
preconditioner may leave a large second-degree defect on wider charts. This
small conditional kernel proposes a finite quadratic C(d), then recomputes
the complete noncommuting interval convolution I-C(d)J(d), through degree four,
and -C(d)R(d), through degree six. The C2*r4 forcing term is retained.

All J0(E), J1(E), and J2(I,E) must be authenticated on the same full curved
predictor/correction domain. All residual coefficients and quartic remainder
must cover that same domain. Midpoint inverse coefficients are proposals only;
no coefficient cancellation is assumed. Actual constructed correction balls
enter the whole image; strict inclusion and weighted contraction below one
remain mandatory on every coordinate. This can re-evaluate retained failed
charts without acquiring new native derivatives, but does not relabel the old
failed attempt. Any resulting connection still requires the full known-root
join and native primitive/frame/positive-angle gates.

Source preparation and synthetic controls only. A separate fixed saved-input
manifest/card, full705 measured arithmetic profile, resource limits and peer
review must precede research-array evaluation. This method does not authorize
