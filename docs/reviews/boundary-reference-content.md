# Boundary and reproducibility reference check — 8 October 2026 UTC

Scope: current manuscript citation contexts, not proof verification or experiment
replication. Five public author PDFs were archived with hashes in attempts003
and004 of `audits/core-reference-content`. Two direct publisher requests returned
403 and remain recorded; the public arXiv versions were used for content.
No authenticated access or browser-challenge workaround was attempted.

## chen2024phases

[Author preprint v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01687): Sections2--3 and Theorem2.1
reduce specified quadratic regression dynamics to a cubic map. The Li--Yorke
chaotic regime can coexist with attracting periodic orbits; it does not mean
every sampled trajectory is chaotic. Orthogonal inputs underlie the decoupled
multi-example examples. The [author publication list](https://xuxingc.github.io/publications/)
confirms TMLR2024, so the bibliography now gives the journal/year. The final
journal PDF was inaccessible; its detailed equivalence to v1 remains open.
Current claims explicitly match the inspected preprint's scope.

## torkamandi2025

[Preprint v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.04286v2), Sections3--5: a95,973-parameter,
two-layer character transformer uses Adam and separate attention/other rates.
Loss criteria define convergence; finite-resolution box counts characterize the
boundary. This is relevant prior transformer evidence, not a full-state orbit
or chaos certificate. The current citation is narrowed accordingly.

## liu2024

[Preprint v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.13971v1), Results/Methods: numerical
learning-rate boundary counts for quadratic objectives with additive or
multiplicative cosine perturbations. It supports a constructed-objective
example, not a universal theorem that nonconvexity forces fractal trainability.

## liang2026

[Preprint v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.25351v3), Sections3--5, Theorem4 and
limitations: convergence regions concern initializations in scalar/vector
factorization; matrix extensions impose orthogonality conditions. Regularization
is central to the described boundary structure. The [author institution](https://www.mis.mpg.de/de/people/guido-montufar/publications)
and indexed official proceedings confirm ICLR2026. The manuscript now separates
initialization-space geometry from learning-rate boundary maps. Detailed identity
of the final proceedings text and archived v3 was not checked.

## ly2025

[Preprint v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.05606v2), deep-network section, Methods
and Extended Data6: empirical riddling-like sensitivity and functional churn
include VGG, ResNet and BERT fine-tuning. These are outcomes of retraining from
nearby initializations; they do not demonstrate chaos during frozen inference.
The minimal-model visualization is explicitly a long transient in Methods.
Current wording distinguishes empirical signatures from a rigorous global-basin
proof. The functional comparison is relevant prior work for any proposed
training-to-deployment robustness contribution.
