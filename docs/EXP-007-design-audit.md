# EXP-007 prospective design and orchestration audit

Date: 2026-09-11. This local audit inspected the new holdout selector,
`prepare_hopfield_holdout.py`, `run_hopfield_holdout.py`, and their synthetic
tests before target execution. No EXP-007 target trajectory, certificate or
preview was generated by this audit. Existing atlas data and the EXP-006
manifest are legitimate disclosed development inputs. Canonical integrity
sections 4 and 4A govern the claim boundary and matched comparisons.

## Confirmed design and data separation

- The selector leaves the original atlas and all margin-defining non-nine
  pixels unchanged. It excludes a closed normalized radius-0.04 halo around
  every EXP-006 starting anchor, then selects four attempts from each margin
  rank third with seed 20260912 and deterministic within-stratum maximin.
  Insufficient candidates fail rather than shrink the exclusion zone.
- There are 3,758 eligible old-map pixels after 956 crop-eligible pixels are
  excluded by the halo. Rank thirds have 1,253/1,253/1,252 pixels. The selected
  minimum development-origin distance is 0.04006168084. These are pre-target
  design facts. Cross-stratum neighbors remain possible, and the halo does not
  exclude all past intervention destinations. Inference is conditional on the
  previously observed atlas, not independent unseen-map generalization.
- The saved plan equals a fresh compilation from the hash-bound old inputs.
  The helper API and prepare call agree, including anchor identities, margin
  directions, 48 retained atlas-radius direction choices, and source binding.
- Fresh orbit phases use indices 337, 1012, 1687 and 2362 of 2,700 saved
  endpoint-excluded samples. Execution uses those exact states and the epoch
  `index*T/2700`. It does not approximate them as exact eighths.
- Both numerical baseline steps start independently from `(0.1,0.1,0.1)`.
  Their period-nine requirement and independent attracting certificate reuse
  the unchanged EXP-006 baseline procedure. Failed anchors remain explicit
  without replacement. The new assay has no long integration for every target.

## Fair directions, explicit preview access and correct score window

All policy actions at a given anchor, phase and dose have the same native
weight norm. Candidates include exact signed axes, signed local and tangent
directions, the original atlas direction, and the uniform angular grid.
Twenty-one candidate entries are retained without deduplication; some vectors
can therefore coincide. Local and tangent policies each inspect two previews;
axes inspect four; uniform search inspects sixteen; augmented search twenty-one.
The atlas uses no preview and the hybrid copies the local decision at radii
through 0.004 or the original atlas direction at 0.016. This switch is explicitly
development-informed and fixed before new outcomes.

`choose_policies` accepts preview records and radius only. It cannot access
tail or full target scores. Policy subsets restrict exactly which forecast
scores can influence each decision. Sign selection uses the first 75 units of
the declared simulator preview, with lowest candidate index breaking exact
ties. The same fine-preview action is used for both final integration steps.
The augmented search consumes atlas and local information; uniform-16 does
not. Its higher candidate budget prevents an equal-total-cost interpretation.

The primary score masks elapsed samples with `times > 75` before fitting the
single global phase. That leaves precisely 2,250 samples from 75.1 through
300.0. The reference epoch is then added, so the cutoff never accidentally
depends on orbit phase. Full-window RMSE is computed separately. The local
variational equation starts with zero sensitivity at elapsed time zero, while
its minimum-direction Gram is recomputed only on the same tail samples. Thus
the local comparator targets the actual new endpoint. Preview and scored
samples are disjoint but dynamically dependent; cloned-state simulator access
is part of the stated capability, not hidden test access.

## Census, source guard and failure review

The target census is `12*4*(4*7+1)*2 = 2784`, including 96 zero-dose shams.
The forecast census before eligibility is `12*4*4*21 = 4032`. Each failed
baseline accounts for 232 target records marked `baseline_not_evaluable` and
336 forecasts that are not evaluable. All scored policies retain failures;
there is no replacement anchor or post-outcome change of comparator.

The runner checks clean committed source, a containing pushed remote ref, and
exact old-source artifact hashes before creating its exclusive attempt
directory. Reuse of an attempt path fails. The 1,800-second watchdog covers
builds, baselines, sensitivities, previews, targets, analysis and verification.
Native failures preserve payloads, while completed traces use the existing
atomic replacement writer. An unhandled sensitivity failure retains its
payload and stops the attempt rather than changing the local policy.

The first audit found one missingness defect: zero-sham means and maxima were
computed over successful shams alone. The prospective fix is verified: both
are now undefined whenever a required sham is incomplete, while successful
and planned counts remain visible. A regression test explicitly removes one
fine-step sham and requires 47/48 coverage with undefined mean and maximum.
The requested `preview-census.json` is also implemented and reconstructed
during verification: it records 4,032 planned forecasts, baseline-dependent
eligible and not-evaluable counts, observed status counts, and the sum of
individual call wall times. That concurrent-call sum is not elapsed runtime.
The collection receipt's elapsed time is captured before final verification;
the 1,800-second watchdog still covers that verification. Both findings were
resolved before freeze.

## Completed checks

Thirteen synthetic tests passed in the final review: six selector tests and
seven runner tests. They cover halo geometry and nonmutation of old margins,
development-score independence, exact halo-boundary exclusion, fresh phase
indices, source binding, duplicate/no-fallback rules, full target IDs, policy
subsets and ties, native direction norms, tail-window masking, sign selection,
ineligible-anchor retention, no success-only primary contrast, and incomplete
zero-sham gating. The preview-helper integration preserves the separate
primary-tail and full-window metric calls.

The independent compiler invocation reproduced the saved plan exactly without
target integration. No design or orchestration blocker remains for the stated
exploratory claim, subject to the runner's source-freeze and push guards. This
audit establishes pre-execution consistency and a
bounded claim, not scientific success. An outcome that fails to beat tangent,
signed-axis or uniform-search controls remains a valid result and cannot be
relabeled as a successful geometric-control transfer.
