## Remaining pre-freeze correction

**`tools/huginn_arc_analysis.py:311–337,355–366` (especially 358–360).**
`admit_item_rows` validates every question/arm cell but does not validate its
`gold_ids_sha256`. `items_cmd` compares the spectrum's `token_ids_sha256` only
with `table[(q, lams[0])]`. The other arms then enter the primary statistic at
lines 369–378 without the same-prefill comparison promised in design lines
69–75 and 185–187.

A concrete source counterexample needs no numerical execution: keep a valid
panel, spectrum and first-arm row unchanged; remove or change only the hash on
one nonfirst-arm row. None of the admission conditions inspects that field,
and the row's residual still enters S. Thus the source does not establish the
promised common prefill for all four correlations. This is an admission gap,
not evidence that the actual producer rows differ. The producer does write the
hash per arm (`huginn_relax_eval.py:486–490`), and its shared gold-ID helper
matches the batch construction in source (`377–403`).

**Minimal fix:** before admitting a question into the common cohort, require
each requested arm's recorded gold hash to equal the spectrum hash. A missing
or unequal hash must refuse admission or give the entire question the declared
token-mismatch disposition in every arm. Add a regression changing/removing
only a nonfirst-arm hash while leaving the baseline and spectrum unchanged.
The existing test at `tests/test_huginn_arc_analysis.py:134–140` changes the
spectrum hash; it does not exercise this case.
