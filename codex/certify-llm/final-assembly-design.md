# Final eight-phase evidence assembly and certificate release

Design only, 13 October 2026 UTC. No final domain, actual evidence admission, model evaluation, derivative campaign, resource allocation or certificate is authorized by this note. Keep all existing evaluators, readers and mathematical gates unchanged. The assembly program would perform artifact admission and rigorous matrix arithmetic only; it would not refine centers or call a transformer.

## Frozen inputs and trust anchors

A reviewed external assembly plan must pin the following independently of the first artifact it reads:

- The original final-domain definition bytes: eight ordered 896-coordinate canonical exact dyadic centers, eight strictly positive exact dyadic radii, the final-box role, exact map recipe and selection provenance. Domain selection is separate from derivative correctness. All 112 shard plans must already have bound this identical definition before their evaluations.
- The complete point-admission plan bytes, successful immediate continuation receipt, original capsule, external parent-audit bytes, and its unchanged numerical seed/prefix pins. Older archive trust is expressly inherited from that externally audited capsule; admission does not claim to rehash unread history.
- The full original numerical seed receipt and seven-array NPZ hash, including exact binary64 A/B proposals and their source pins, through the same base plan used by point admission. The original seed centers are never substituted for the final exact centers. A/B need not be derivatives at the final centers or a proved inverse: the root gate measures their defects.
- All 112 successful, untimed-out final derivative attempt paths and receipt hashes, explicitly assigned to phase 0–7 and starts 0,64,...,832. No prototype, failed shard, selected-column estimate or replacement picked during assembly may count. The reference count and assignment are checked before expensive admission; the existing collector independently requires every one of the 8×896 positive signed coordinate basis columns exactly once.
- Independently reconstructed map/assets/config/steering-vector/prefix-ID/constants identities, full state/vocabulary dimensions, and separate point and derivative backend/prefix contracts. Points use the reviewed 2048-bit prefix. Derivatives use the reviewed 1024-bit prefix stage, whose overall historical attempt later failed at an unrelated box diagnostic. The latter is not a successful derivative attempt, and the former cannot replace its cache.
- An explicit complete source dictionary: the eventual assembly wrapper; every `point_evidence.SOURCES` entry; all derivative evidence/collector/evaluator dependencies; `shooting_certificate.py`, `shooting_gates.py`, `proof_gates.py`; the numerical-proposal reader/converter; and any newly introduced persistence/admission helpers. Expand and record the transitive dependency list in the frozen plan. A single top-level script digest is insufficient. Package/backend and declared arithmetic contexts are separate pins.
- Source-bound mathematical/evaluator reviews and the exact theorem obligations below. Hashes authenticate bytes; they do not prove a review, implementation or producer declaration true. Any Git-commit/blob verification must be performed and recorded separately from the existing readers' captured-runtime-source verification.

Use a new fresh owned attempt, a privately pushed source/run card, and a fixed plan hash. Start the absolute deadline before imports or artifact admission. No automatic retries, precision changes, radius changes, altered proposal matrices or reduced gate set occur in the attempt. An adaptation requires a new disclosed plan and retains the failed attempt.

## Conditional mathematical gates

Call unchanged `shooting_certificate.check_shooting_certificate` once with the admitted native centers, exact nominal radii, full exact-center images, eight full whole-box Jacobians, exact dyadic A/B proposals, and bound contract. Freeze `bound_method='factored'`, `powers=(1,2)` and any positive stability weights explicitly; the simplest initial design uses no weights. The assembly precision is separately frozen after the resource pilot is assessed. Do not silently switch to a looser default power list.

Retain all three conditional results, including failures:

- **Root:** every weighted contraction and strict inclusion row passes on the same full product box. This gives one ordered zero of H_i(x)=F(x_i)−x_(i+1) in that product box. The contraction bound also makes the fixed square preconditioner nonsingular, so a fixed point of x−RH(x) is a genuine root. It does not exclude other F^8 fixed points in X_0 whose intermediate phases leave the other boxes.
- **Primitive period:** the original nominal phase boxes X_0 and X_d are strictly separated at each proper divisor d=1,2,4. Once the shooting root exists, its d-th phase lies in X_d; no assertion that F^d(X_0) lies in X_d is needed. Touching boxes fail.
- **Attraction:** form the signed whole-box interval product J_7 J_6 ... J_0 in precisely that order. This encloses the derivative of F^8 at the root. Require an outward induced-norm bound below one for at least one of the frozen powers 1 or 2. Root-finding contraction is not a substitute. The conclusion is local attraction, not trapping of the entire nominal product box.

Analytic/evaluator obligations must establish that the same fixed exact-real map is C1 on neighborhoods of all nominal boxes; every constant, weight and elementary operation is rigorously enclosed; divisions/normalizations are valid; images enclose F(c_i); and each J_i encloses DF on the entire nominal X_i, including all transverse directions. Scalar points, streamed all-vocabulary Jets, prefix reuse and mixed precision rely on their explicitly pinned reviewed implementations and backend arithmetic contract. Numerical model agreement, center convergence, complete hashes, synthetic tests and a Boolean producer status cannot alone establish these obligations.

Optional strict all-vocabulary label margins may establish labels on containing receiver boxes and therefore on the proved orbit phases once existence is established. Label agreement is a separate claim with its own bound evidence, never a substitute for any orbit gate. This final calculation alone establishes neither global shrimp organization nor a training or practical language-model benefit.

## Outcomes, independent audit and release

The assembly attempt always writes `certificate_passed:false`, including when `conditional_gates_passed:true`. Preserve a compact admission/gate summary before streaming the full conditional result into a `.partial` artifact and atomically marking it complete. A mathematical gate failure is an inconclusive sufficient-condition result, not evidence of nonexistence. Authentication failure, timeout, memory failure and unfinished serialization are different statuses. None can be promoted by reading an earlier partial success field.

A separate post-run audit is the release boundary. It must be performed independently of the producing run and saved as an immutable manifest with its evidence files, source-bound review scope and concrete findings. It rechecks the exact plan/candidate/source/backend/enclosure bindings, completeness of original artifacts and native decoding, every strict gate row and monodromy order, the analytic/evaluator hypotheses, process completion, and full report serialization. Audit recomputation must be described honestly: do not claim a full arithmetic replay if only individual inequalities and bound records were independently checked. Any unread older capsule bytes remain covered by the explicit already-pinned audit trust boundary.

A small later finalization plan externally pins **both** the completed assembly receipt/report and the independent audit manifest. This two-stage construction avoids circular hashes: the assembly plan need not know its future audit hash, and neither the producer nor its self-declared metadata chooses the audit that will validate it. Merely naming an audit file or setting `approved:true` is not adequate; the authorized independent review must actually discharge the stated obligations for those exact artifacts and source versions. The finalizer's own complete source and tests also require review.

Only after all those external obligations are established, both adapters and full coverage succeed, all three conditional gates pass with strict witnesses, and the independent result audit has no unresolved blocker may a final release record set `certificate_passed:true`. Its statement is scoped to the specified frozen nonlinear feedback map: an existing primitive period-8 orbit, unique as an ordered shooting-root tuple in the specified product box and locally attracting. It is a computer-assisted proof with explicitly documented trusted arithmetic/evaluator and audit assumptions, not a proof-assistant verification. Before that boundary the result is `conditional_gates_passed` with independent review pending, and cannot be described as a completed certificate.

## Resources and failure retention

No target-size runtime or RAM ceiling is selected here. Root's separate synthetic full-mantissa gate pilot is intended to measure the unchanged factored/powers-(1,2) path including serialization; even its completed measurement is an engineering workload, not an upper bound for every final input. Include admission/hashing/native reconstruction costs in a subsequently reviewed concrete plan. There are 6,422,528 Jacobian entries and 112 original shard inventories. Streaming shard JSON avoids one large avoidable allocation; it does not eliminate eight native matrices, signed product temporaries, or the current attraction helper's 2,408,448 retained ball records for the monodromy and two powers.

Use a fresh task-owned process group, CPU/thread envelope, hard address-space limit and external wall-time watchdog fixed before admission. Record stage start/completion, measured elapsed time/RSS, actual exit status and verified owned-process termination. Save plan/source identity, each completed adapter report, exact admission manifest and compact gate summary as soon as available. Preserve partially written artifacts and an interrupted-stage marker on failure. The unchanged gate call has no per-matrix persistence hook; if interrupted inside it, do not fabricate a completed root/attraction result. A later optimization that adds stage checkpoints or changes report storage requires separate source review and a new attempt.
