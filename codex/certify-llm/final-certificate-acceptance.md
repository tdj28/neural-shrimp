# Finite acceptance criteria for the final Qwen P8 certificate

13 October 2026 UTC. Completion checklist only; no target computation or certificate is reported here. This closes the five evidence obligations in `final-evaluator-obligations-review.md` without adding a proof-assistant requirement.

The accepted standard is a computer-assisted proof under the explicitly trusted Python/FLINT/Arb arithmetic and controlled execution stack: reviewed mathematical formulas and source, authenticated actual execution, and an independent audit of original endpoints and saved scalar witnesses. Hashes establish identity, not mathematical truth. A second implementation of the complete transformer or a second dense matrix replay is not a new mandatory gate unless a concrete discrepancy calls the reviewed computation into question.

The independent audit must bind the completed assembly plan, source, receipt, admission manifest and full report to its own immutable audit manifest. It checks every strict root/primitive/attraction witness and original endpoint binding, records any inherited older audit authority, and has no unresolved substantive discrepancy. `witness_audit.py` checks rational bound directions and scalar consistency, including saved power-matrix row norms. It **does not recompute** dense products, preconditioned residual products or establish evaluator enclosures. Those computations remain supported by the reviewed source and authenticated successful Arb execution. The audit must state this scope rather than describe the scalar checker as an arithmetic replay.

Once all five rows and that independent audit pass, a separate release record can set `certificate_passed:true`, externally pinning the assembly and independent audit manifest. It may be a directly reviewed record or a separately reviewed minimal finalizer; no further speculative infrastructure is required. Until then, assembly and helper records remain `certificate_passed:false`, even if their conditional gates pass.

The released statement is exactly: the specified frozen nonlinear expected-embedding feedback map has a primitive period-8 orbit, unique as an ordered shooting root in the stated product box, and locally attracting. It does not claim uniqueness of every F^8 fixed point in the first box, trapping of the entire box, global shrimp organization, ordinary token-generation behavior or training utility. Token labels require separate strict all-vocabulary margins; they are not required for the orbit certificate. A failed sufficient-condition gate is retained as an inconclusive setup result and requires a disclosed new attempt, not stronger wording.
