## Three distinct scalar procedures

The original procedure uses the declared capped power-polynomial degree, selects the phase with smallest in-sample relative residual, and retains its logarithmic derivative estimate divided by the observed symbolic period. Its historical value-fit threshold is residual below `0.01`; numerical full rank is recorded rather than silently added as an original selection rule.

The amended procedure fits Chebyshev polynomials on the fixed candidate grid, selects the degree by fivefold random-CV value error, retains phases with CV relative error below `0.01`, then takes their median per-step exponent. It uses the original producer logarithm floor and the producer's physical-coordinate imaginary-root filter for the direct comparison. This is the producer's disclosed outcome-informed amendment, not a new prespecified confirmation.

The own purged-contiguous procedure retains the same candidate grid and value-error threshold but uses contiguous folds with adjacent pairs removed from training. It additionally requires full rank in every selected fold and the final fit, and uses the unfloored derivative-log estimate, preserving undefined cases. This extra rank qualification is explicit. Temporal purging is a finite sensitivity check; it does not make the trajectory independent or prove derivative accuracy from value-fit error.

The shape columns count cells with at least one retained selected fit of the stated type. They do not say every phase is unimodal. A single stationary point does not determine whether the fitted curve has a maximum or minimum, and the original 59-count must not be described as 59 quadratic maxima. The maximum classification additionally uses derivative signs and nonzero curvature. These are properties of the fitted finite scalar curves, not proofs about an invariant full-state return map.

## Interpretation and remaining scope

The saved-data stage reproduces the descriptive 49/62 and 51/62 summaries while exposing the original stationary-point/maximum distinction, rank warnings and persistent outliers. It supports a useful finite scalar representation in many sampled cells. It does not show that the observation is injective, that the full state lies on an invariant one-dimensional curve, or that the fitted map is smoothly conjugate to the full dynamics. Even exact scalar-factor behavior and agreement of the largest exponent can coexist with additional expanding dimensions.

These are exploratory analyses on the producer's same finite sequences, after disclosed access to the original and amended summaries. The amended method was developed after inspecting outliers. The saved full-state twin exponents and state-period labels are inputs, not independently regenerated observations in this all-cell stage. No fresh confirmatory sample, asymptotic chaos proof, perpetual token repetition, causal behavioral mechanism or compute-saving claim follows.

The independent primary trajectory has its own completed report and actual-evidence acceptance. This all-65 addendum is now complete. The optional independent stress runs and their future saved-evidence checks remain separate; their outcomes are not present in this report.
