## What the saved contract can support

The source runs the fixed-start, one-soft-slot map for 1,000 transient and 7,000 recorded updates by default. It groups states at `ph::q`, with q supplied as7; it does not independently detect a seven-band clock or save token IDs. Each phase is centred independently, given its own SVD leading direction, and projected to `s`. The return pairs are `s[:-1],s[1:]`; for the declared complete record this means 1,000 phase samples and999 seven-step pairs per phase. Actual lengths, cell order, args, completion and archive authority must be checked before adopting these expected counts.

The NPZ saves only cells, period and each `c{cell}_p{phase}` scalar sequence. The JSON saves first/second explained-variance shares, spread and producer turning count. Native states, PCA vectors/means and singular values are not persisted by this source. Thus an independent saved-data pass can reconstruct all scalar pairs, spreads, binned counts and alternative turning diagnostics; explained-variance shares can only be retained as producer fields unless another original artifact supplies the missing inputs. It cannot independently refit PCA, verify all-state band separation, establish effective precision from arrays alone, verify the actual symbolic clock, or prove native conjugacy. These are finite evidence limits, not reasons to request model replay.

The literal producer counter uses40 equal-width x bins, keeps bins with at least5 observations, takes median y, deletes zero successive slopes and counts remaining sign changes. It connects retained bins even across unoccupied gaps. This is an exact reproducible descriptive count, not a reliable intrinsic turning-point estimator. Preserve it and every omitted-bin/flat-slope disposition; do not silently replace its reported3–13 counts with an alternate favorable result.

Each PCA direction has arbitrary sign: changing `s` to `−s` preserves the number of extrema but interchanges maxima and minima. A one-extremum statement should therefore be orientation-independent. The specific phase0–3 minima /4–6 maxima describes the saved coordinate convention, not an invariant of the map.

## Prospective finite method to predeclare

A useful own method should expose scale dependence rather than choose one smoothing/prominence threshold after observing the records. The following compact design is suitable for a later source/card review; it is not execution approval.

1. **Keep the complete paired observations.** Validate every saved sequence, cell and phase; retain nonfinite/constant/duplicate-x cases and their counts, without a finite fill. Normalize x and y only by their respective actual finite ranges, while retaining physical-coordinate extrema and all extremum witnesses. A zero range makes normalized diagnostics undefined. Keep temporal indices and do not shuffle or identify these dependent samples as IID noise.

2. **Use a fixed multiscale conditional-quantile description.** A prospective grid such as16,24,32,48,64 bins can be declared once for every cell/phase, with both equal-width and equal-count partitions. Ties must not be split inconsistently, and occupied runs separated by an empty x interval must remain separate rather than bridged. Record every bin boundary, count, median x/y and y quantiles. Retain all scales and any underpopulated/undefined bins; do not select the scale that returns one extremum. This is a proposed finite resolution family, not a pass threshold.

3. **Report the entire extremum-prominence profile.** On each occupied median-curve run, identify all interior extrema with an explicit plateau rule; endpoints are not interior turning points. Record their x locations, type and adjacent vertical excursions, and a deterministic adjacent-extremum cancellation hierarchy ordered by vertical prominence, with fixed left-index tie-breaking. Report the number and locations that remain at every distinct observed prominence breakpoint, in absolute and range-normalized units. No binary “unimodal” decision is assigned by choosing a prominence cutoff after outcomes. This shows whether there is one large-scale extremum plus small variations, or several comparably prominent turns, and how that description depends on binning. Any eventual finite descriptive summary must name its scale; it cannot prove a continuum map has one turning point.

4. **Expose doubled strands separately from the median curve.** Conditional widths and quantile fans must accompany any turning profile; a median can hide two branches. Keep even/odd successive return-visit labels as a fixed, separately reported diagnostic of period-doubled strands, showing their conditional locations and counts at every declared scale. Swapping the start parity only swaps labels. Nonseparation by parity does not rule out other multivalued structure, and separation alone does not establish a two-dimensional invariant set. Any local two-cluster/gap diagnostic, if adopted, must be defined prospectively and show every cell/phase, not be tuned only on phases4–5. It should remain descriptive, with slope, sparse occupancy and projection folding as alternative explanations. A single-valued scalar-map claim cannot be inferred solely from the binned median or a high PCA variance share.

5. **Preserve finite variation and all claims' denominators.** Compare every phase and all seven cells, while keeping body-edge, selected figure and hostile-line groups distinct. A fixed first/second-half check of the saved pairs may describe temporal stability but is not an independent replicate. Preserve all literal producer differences, quantitative diagnostics and undefined cases. Retain disclosed producer summary exposure; this is independent unblinded saved-data analysis. Do not use a successful alternate diagnostic to erase the original counter's limitations.

This design deliberately avoids a new significance test, fitted dynamical model, bootstrap calibration or numerical unimodality pass threshold.
