# Task21a prospective independent saved-arithmetic design

This is CPU-only arithmetic on hash-bound original records. It imports no producer code and calls no model, eigensolver, Newton solver or continuation. Source/schema inspection and disclosed producer summaries precede this preparation. Inference owns complete immutable input/receipt admission, including the explicit finite local c completion basis; local d remains excluded, and all execution/Git actions. This preparation grants no permission to read unfinished outputs or launch an audit.

Every attempt has a distinct identifier, exact source/command/temperature/period metadata, original terminal status, and separately pinned available cascade.json, superstable.json, progress.jsonl or diagnostic log. Missing files and unavailable histories remain explicit. Attempt failure and scientific results already saved before failure are different fields. There is no default root, inferred missing level or automatic replacement by a different attempt.

The checker reads full JSON numeric spellings. It preserves both the complete decimal spelling and the represented binary64 value/hex form. Original point ratios are independently recomputed using ordinary binary64 differences/divisions for a literal producer comparison; every nonzero difference is retained. A second calculation uses exact rational arithmetic on the printed decimal values. Reported interval endpoints use explicit rational floor/ceiling rounding, not formatting to nearest. These arithmetic enclosures concern saved scalar inputs only, not model rounding or uncertainty from missing directions.

For every accepted flip row, retain period, parameter, all saved multiplier pairs, critical multiplier, next modulus, full Newton history, Ritz residual/history and each locator evaluation. Check finite values, the least-real-part multiplier association, real -1 classification, the declared original best-|mu+1| threshold, critical Ritz threshold, next modulus < 1, and the original period-dependent Newton tolerance. The starting orbit is not automatically half-period checked; later calls have source-level half-period refusal, but the actual split values are not saved in the flip record. Those distinctions must be reported. Locator evaluations do not include the initial a/b endpoints, so they do not reconstruct the final bracket by themselves. An accepted best evaluation need not be the final midpoint. No signed interval is manufactured from a single accepted point.

For every superstable band, retain detected period, any original error, the reported point estimate, all evaluation clean flags, the ordered or reverse-oriented clean bracket, and its two signed multipliers. The saved record belongs to the positive clean endpoint, not to the interpolated root. Its leading multiplier is a different role from Evaluation.record's least-real-part critical multiplier; do not conflate them. Recompute the secant estimate, validate opposite signs and bracket membership, and check saved cleanliness comparisons where the available fields permit them. Missing per-evaluation imaginary components, Newton histories, half-period distances or residuals remain explicitly unavailable. 'Stopped at the transverse spectrum' is an interpolated estimate with its complete clean bracket, not a root directly evaluated to zero.

Ratios are formed only from three consecutive doubled periods in an explicit source-bound sequence. Each individual attempt is tabulated first. A composite sequence must list exact attempt IDs and period selectors before execution; duplicate observations at a shared period remain separate with their full signed difference. Any missing, duplicated or mismatched level refuses that composite rather than silently skipping it. Original producer arrays that skip failed bands are compared literally and separately; they cannot authorize a cross-gap scientific ratio.

For R=(b-a)/(c-b), clean-bracket sensitivity is computed only when the three intervals are strictly ordered. The shared middle value is used consistently. Since R increases with b and decreases with a and c, the lower corner is (a_hi,b_lo,c_hi), and the upper corner is (a_lo,b_hi,c_lo). Overlapping intervals or a denominator that can vanish produce an unresolved/unbounded disposition. This is a numerical bracket sensitivity envelope conditional on the recorded brackets representing the intended continuous branch; it is not a rigorous enclosure of the exact model root.

P-F1 uses the frozen first flip ratio and strict comparison with 2.844 at both temperatures, plus the stated between-temperature ordering. A missing first ratio remains unscorable; a failed component at -0.156 can falsify the conjunction without inventing the -0.152 ratio. P-F2 uses the last two computed **superstable** ratios at the declared scoring time. 'Within 5% of 4.669' means the exact two-sided interval [4.43555,4.90245]; the parenthetical 'at least 4.436' is rounded shorthand, not a replacement rule. Point-estimate pass and whole-bracket-envelope containment are reported independently. The 06:05 score has period192 as its latest completed level; the promised but then unfinished period384 cannot be retroactively substituted.

For crescent-t-0.156-e, the original locator refusal remains a refusal. A separately labelled reconstruction fits a centered least-squares line to every admitted logged evaluation of the declared period. It retains all points, residuals, absolute maxima and point ranges. It reports the full root, slope, residual RMS/sample scatter, maximum absolute line residual divided by |slope|, and an optional standard error from the local linear model. The latter assumes fixed x, a valid local linear approximation and independent homoscedastic errors; deterministic correlated solver noise does not establish those assumptions or a calibrated confidence interval. Both logged g and Re(mu)+1 consistency are retained. A fixed near-root subset, if requested, is only a sensitivity check and never replaces the all-point fit. Parameter-span and explicit ±1e-12 sensitivity ratios remain separate from a fitted standard-error interval. None promotes the failed locator to original success.

The --g-accept implementation checks only the best single |mu+1| against its declared cutoff. The FBD2 log's claim that excessive scatter automatically triggers refusal is not implemented. Any scatter-based interpretation is an additional descriptive diagnostic. At -0.152, half-period collapse and a finite-step forward search without a flip support a bubble-compatible finite record and 'no further flip found at the stated resolution'; they do not exclude a narrower cascade between sampled points. Unlogged intermediate multipliers cannot supply a closest approach to -1.
