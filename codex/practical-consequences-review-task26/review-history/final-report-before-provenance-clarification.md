## Corrections to make first

1. **Separate generated answers from autonomous-loop readouts.** REPRO1 compares the most-probable-token readout of a continuous expected-embedding state. These tokens are not fed back as emitted text. Its tail-disagreement flag means at least one disagreement during the final 500 steps, not disagreement at the final step or permanent disagreement. REPRO-T separately compares canonical parsed answers and raw answer text in a fixed growing-context task. Do not interchange the two kinds of evidence.
2. **Preserve the historical forecast and name its future-data dependence.** The original REPRO1 plot used the smallest positive distance anywhere in the recorded trajectory. It is a retrospective scaling comparison. The independent first-positive-distance calculation is the stated observable-seed alternative. Both calculations and both excluded noncrossers remain reported; their numerical agreement does not make the original calculation prospective.
3. **Report medians and quantiles as such.** REPRO-T's finite perturbation-growth median of about 0.033 is not an upper bound: its T=1 rows extend to about 0.241. For REPRO1, the periodic float64 linear 95th percentile of final distance is below 3e-12, but only 182/192 observations, 94.7917%, are literally at most 3e-12. Do not convert an interpolated quantile into an empirical coverage guarantee.
4. **Qualify the short-task comparator and test unit.** The forced 32-token greedy comparator followed by a fixed cue and eight answer tokens had zero held-out accuracy. It is not an ordinary early-stopping greedy accuracy baseline. The 96 indexed instances contain 95 distinct ordered and 94 distinct unordered additions; keep the frozen cohort and disclose development/distractor overlaps and the unresolved identity of the earlier held-out smoke row.
5. **Report the failed crossover criteria beside the positive average effect.** The all-cell validation passes only 23/24 and fails its original rule. The signed-mean readout-only versus full-arm discrepancy is 0.2286, above the frozen 0.1 tolerance, so the original joint sufficiency criterion fails. This does not erase the positive average readout effect or imply that the saved data are invalid.
6. **Correct the stored-interval transcriptions.** The frozen prose does not match the actual saved intervals. Recommended outward-enclosing two-decimal displays are R1 readout [1.78, 3.59], R1 network [−0.23, 0.12], and R2 readout [0.91, 1.93]. Also display the XO interaction as [0.09, 0.45] if an enclosing interval is intended. Exact endpoints, ordinary-rounding comparisons and the explicit reporting-only amendment are preserved in the companion files.
7. **Keep the finite mechanism conclusion within its tested scope.** Crossed tangent cocycles along saved steered orbits separate derivative contributions there. They do not establish attractors of autonomous crossed systems or rule out relocation of the visited states. The stored readout statistic is estimated intrinsic dimension of a PSD covariance operator, not its stable rank. The output-projected vector Wv is not the end-to-end logit derivative of an interior-layer intervention.

The numerical summaries below display approximate estimates; exact endpoints and any recommended enclosing bounds are identified in the component reports and interval-display amendment002.
