## Reproducibility paragraph and table

1. Consequences lines 13–15 and 52–56: the additional rows reverse steering vectors but leave temperatures in their original order. Replace “the same cells in reverse order” with “additional rows with reversed steering vectors and the original temperature order.” The first 448 target rows remain paired. Prefix caches are recomputed at each batch shape, so the scope includes their rounding as well as later updates. This is a description correction, not a request to rerun the experiment.

2. Lines 16–23: the recorded threshold is a strictly greater than 0.01 relative soft-state distance. A median threshold-crossing step cannot be called a median time to order-one separation. Pending arithmetic, say “190 of the 192 source-labelled chaotic cells crossed relative distance 0.01, at a median of 102 steps among those crossing.” If order-one separation is separately supported by later maxima/final distances, report that as a distinct observation with its own count/time.

3. Lines 20–23 and figure caption: the historical forecast calculation used the minimum positive distance over the entire trajectory, not the first positive distance. That minimum may contain future information. The independent audit will retain the published calculation and separately report the stated first-positive definition on the same cohort. Do not silently replace one or call the historical minimum-based relationship a prospective forecast. Retain both censored cells and distinguish the correlation conditional on crossing from predictive validation.

4. Lines 17–18, 26, 63 and 82–85: these are most-probable-token readouts of a continuous expected-embedding update, not emitted or sampled text. The table's statistic is a cell having any disagreeing readout within the final 500 steps. Prefer column heading “any tail readout disagreement” and say “most-probable-token readouts differ at least once in the final 500 steps.” Keep final-step disagreement and the per-cell tail disagreement fraction distinct.

5. Lines 18–20: a median of maxima over noncrossing periodic cells is not an all-periodic bound. A linear 95th percentile and the literal fraction of 192 cells under a stated number are not identical statements. The saved-array audit will retain both with exact denominators and outward bounds. 6. Lines 28–31: the inherited labels are from float64 source maps. They do not establish the regimes of the lower-precision maps. In the original REPRO1 loading order, a class-level RMSNorm patch installed for float64 persists into the later bfloat16 model, while the expected-embedding table/state remain float32. Describe this measured implementation explicitly; “a common serving precision” must not imply all ordinary bfloat16 serving implementations behave identically. Do not transfer this load-order effect to REPRO-T, which constructs bfloat16 first.

7. Lines 33–45: retain the growing-context soft-thinking pipeline distinction and the forced-length greedy comparator. Prefer “greedy comparator with the same forced length” over “ordinary greedy decoding.” The primary endpoint is canonical first-integer answer disagreement; saved raw-text disagreement is separate. Saved source flags do not independently reconstruct unsaved complete token sequences/trajectories. The 96 indexed held-out examples include a duplicate and development/distractor overlaps; one unidentified held-out example was used in the disclosed smoke test. These do not authorize changing the frozen set, but they qualify “held-out” and prevent an implication of 96 wholly independent unseen problems.

8. Lines 40–48: the exact sign-flip estimator and attainable resolution should be named. A plus-one estimate from 10,000 randomizations has minimum 1/10,001, conventionally rounded to 10^-4; a raw zero-exceedance estimator is zero. The unavailable producer inline implementation cannot be reconstructed from a printed p-value. The independent audit freezes and reports both. The six-example finite perturbation check supports a limited implementation explanation, not exclusion of all chaotic or nonlinear amplification in every bfloat16 context. Suggested concluding scope: “In this short, fixed pipeline, batch-dependent low-precision differences propagate through continuous feedback and alter extracted answers. The float64 perturbation check does not show the same answer instability over this budget. This is distinct from the longer-horizon autonomous-loop divergence experiment.”

## Mechanism paragraph

9. The product of the finite-twin network/readout gains telescopes algebraically, with the recorded normalization boundary term. These are finite secant factors; distinguish them from exact infinitesimal Lyapunov factors and from a local operator-norm bound. The fixed-iteration Rayleigh estimate does not certify the largest eigenvalue, and small successive change does not exclude convergence to a subleading mode. The saved convergence summary covers the first min(record,1000) steps, not all 1500.

10. “Stable rank” is misnamed for trace(A)/r1. For a positive semidefinite covariance operator, trace(A)/||A|| is intrinsic dimension (effective rank), whereas stable rank(A) is ||A||_F^2/||A||^2. Rename the plotted quantity and retain its approximate fixed-iteration denominator. A fresh numerical experiment is unnecessary for the terminology correction. Definition source: Martinsson and Tropp, *Randomized Numerical Linear Algebra*, section 2.6, equations 2.1–2.2, https://tropp.caltech.edu/books/MT20-Randomized-Numerical-arXiv-v3.pdf.

11. The readout bias Wv is the output projection of the chosen vector. For a vector injected at an interior transformer layer, it is not the actual end-to-end derivative of logits with respect to that intervention. Replace “direct logit effect of the steering vector” with “output-projected direction Wv.” A pure logit bias leaves the network function unchanged, but changes visited states and therefore the evaluated network Jacobians.

12. Replace “A crossover at fixed states rules this out” with “A crossover at fixed states separates the two derivative contributions along the recorded steered orbits.” The crossed tangent cocycles need not be derivatives along trajectories of autonomous crossed systems. A positive crossed growth rate is not independently a chaotic attractor. This leaves room for the high-temperature state-relocation interpretation already present later in the paragraph.

13. The original XO card requires arm 11 to match the twin exponent within 0.02 at every cell. The producer reports one failure among 24 and a changed development-cell regime; the actual audit must retain both, not call the aggregate validation passed. The original closeness-based sufficiency criterion also failed by overshoot. A retrospective mechanistic interpretation can remain useful, but the paper should state that this predefined sufficient criterion failed, alongside the descriptive contrasts. A rounding-floor explanation for the failed twin check should be distinguished from an independent validation of that explanation.

14. Readout-dominated expansion is scoped to the sampled model/direction and low-temperature conditions. The high-temperature network-expansion examples are exceptions to a universal readout rule and should appear adjacent to the main conclusion. Suggested leading sentence: “For these low-temperature philosophy-steered examples, the measured expansion is primarily associated with the readout contribution; the network contribution damps it. At higher temperature, network expansion and the states visited also matter.” Avoid “steering thus makes the loop chaotic” as a universal causal conclusion.

15. Row-matched observational contrasts use chaotic-cell-count weighting and resample temperature rows. This differs from equal weighting of all rows/cells and from randomized intervention inference. Preserve finite-value exclusions and any overlap between chaotic and near-zero groups. A within-row label permutation assumes exchangeability; it does not establish a causal readout intervention. The logit-bias experiment supports existence of finite protocol-qualified chaotic examples without residual steering, not a claim that a failed bias experiment would establish network changes as necessary.
