## Concrete source/report distinctions

1. **Name the spectral quantity correctly.** `steer_gain.py:20,134-139` computes `trace(A)/r1`, with `r1` a power-iteration Rayleigh estimate of the largest eigenvalue of the tied-embedding positive-semidefinite readout derivative. With an exact leading eigenvalue, this is the intrinsic dimension of A, not the conventional stable rank of A, which is `||A||_F^2 / ||A||_2^2`. It also equals the stable rank of the positive square root of A, but that is not what the current prose names. Prefer 'estimated readout intrinsic dimension (trace divided by estimated leading eigenvalue)' in the figure/methods, preserving all saved values. This terminology is supported by [Martinsson and Tropp, *Randomized Numerical Linear Algebra*, section2.6, equations2.1-2.2](https://tropp.caltech.edu/books/MT20-Randomized-Numerical-arXiv-v3.pdf). The author-hosted primary source was checked on15October; no new bibliography entry is required merely to fix the name.
2. **The denominator is estimated.** `readout_stats` performs a fixed number of power products, with a warm-started vector and successive Rayleigh-quotient change. A small successive change is not a general guarantee that the leading eigenvalue has been found. Calling the saved value a rigorous leading-norm bound is unsupported. Moreover `max_rayleigh_change` at line164 is a maximum over the stored first `min(record,1000)` steps, not over every recorded step when record exceeds1000. State that coverage and keep the original diagnostic; no eigensolver rerun is required.
3. **Exact factorization does not make every factor a derivative norm.** For nonzero finite twin differences the product of the two relative secant factors is an algebraic identity, with the recorded endpoint boundary term. The separate code comment `g_read <= r1_rel` is not a guaranteed inequality between a finite secant and the local base-state derivative, especially when `r1` is only an estimate. The true local derivative norm bounds infinitesimal directional growth at that point; a finite secant generally needs control along its segment. Report the measured twin factors under their finite-offset scope, keeping exact algebra separate from asymptotic exponent interpretation.
4. **The logit bias is a constructed intervention.** The code constructs `b=Wv` from a vector added after an intermediate block, then centers and normalizes it. It is a projected logit direction; it is not generally the full derivative of the later blocks and final normalization with respect to that intermediate-layer intervention. Replace 'the direct logit effect of the steering vector' by 'the output-projected steering direction Wv, centered and normalized'. The successful bias condition, if the saved audit confirms it, supports 'a readout-only logit-bias intervention produced cells meeting the finite chaos criterion with the residual-network function unchanged'. It does not by itself isolate token competition as the unique mediator or prove the residual-network response irrelevant at all temperatures.
5. **Fixed-function and fixed-trajectory claims differ.** With alpha=0 and a nonzero beta, the network function is unchanged, but the orbit and hence its visited network Jacobians can change. An orbit-preserving crossover measures derivative growth along a prescribed steered orbit; hybrid derivatives are not necessarily derivatives along trajectories of an autonomous hybrid model. A positive hybrid exponent does not itself prove that the corresponding standalone hybrid model has a chaotic attractor. Review actual crossover source/evidence in26(d) before numerical verdicts. The paper's 'rules this out' (excerpt line23) is too absolute and conflicts with its high-temperature orbit-relocation explanation (line30). Prefer 'tests the contribution of each derivative factor at the same recorded states'.
6. **Keep the observed exception adjacent to the headline.** The same paragraph later identifies expanding network/contracting readout cases and direction/size differences. Replace an unrestricted claim that steering makes chaos by turning readout contraction into expansion with a statement restricted to the sampled low-temperature regimes, then describe the observed exceptions. This is a scope correction, not a request for new experiments.
